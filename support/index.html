简体中文

AssetBoard（聚沙）是一款本地优先的全球资产追踪工具：你的持仓、价格与盈亏全部保存在设备本地，不上传任何服务器，无账号、无云同步、无广告、不采集个人信息。

如何开始

  • 首次打开即可一键加载示例数据，体验全部界面与图表（不与真实资产混淆，可随时清空）。
  • 在「设置 → 添加资产」中按市场添加：A 股 / 美股 / 港股 / 基金 / 黄金 / 期货 / 加密货币等。
  • 行情默认手动刷新；可在「设置 → 刷新」中选择定时或实时模式。

常见问题

我的数据安全吗？
安全。所有数据仅存于本机，App 不联网上传任何个人财务信息，也没有第三方统计 SDK。

支持哪些市场与币种？
A 股、美股、港股、日股、基金、黄金、期货、加密货币；计价支持人民币（CNY）、港币（HKD）、美元（USD）多币种汇总。

桌面小组件为什么不实时？
受系统调度限制，Widget 为定时刷新，非逐秒实时；刷新间隔可在设置中调整。

需要登录吗？
不需要。v1.0 无账号体系，开箱即用。

English

AssetBoard is a local-first global asset tracker: your holdings, prices and P&L live on your device only — no server upload, no account, no cloud sync, no ads, no personal-data collection.

Getting Started

  • On first launch, load sample data with one tap to explore every screen and chart (kept separate from real holdings, clearable anytime).
  • Add assets by market in Settings → Add Asset: A-shares / US / HK / Japan stocks, funds, gold, futures, crypto, and more.
  • Quotes refresh manually by default; choose scheduled or live mode in Settings → Refresh.

FAQ

Is my data safe?
Yes. Everything stays on device. The app uploads no financial information and ships no third-party analytics SDK.

Which markets & currencies?
A-shares, US, HK, Japan stocks, funds, gold, futures, crypto; multi-currency totals in CNY / HKD / USD.

Why isn't the widget real-time?
System scheduling makes the Widget a periodic refresh, not second-by-second; the interval is configurable in Settings.

Do I need to sign in?
No. v1.0 has no account system — open and use.
