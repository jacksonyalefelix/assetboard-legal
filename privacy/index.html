隐私政策（简体中文）

一句话概括：我们不收集您的任何数据。AssetBoard（聚沙）是本地优先（local-first）的资产组合追踪应用，我们没有服务器，也不运营任何后端服务。您的持仓、交易记录与资产数据只保存在您自己的设备上 —— 我们既无法访问，也不会传输给任何人。

一、我们不收集的信息

本应用不要求注册或登录，也不收集任何可直接或间接识别您身份的信息。具体包括：

  • 不收集姓名、手机号码、电子邮箱、证件号码等身份信息；
  • 不采集任何设备标识符（如 IDFV、IDFA），不进行跨 App 或跨网站追踪；
  • 不集成任何第三方统计、分析、广告或崩溃上报 SDK；
  • 不申请定位、相机、相册、麦克风、通讯录、通知等系统敏感权限（本应用未声明任何此类权限）；
  • 不收集您的持仓、成交记录、成本价、资产总额等任何财务数据。

二、数据保存在哪里

  • 全部数据（持仓、交易记录、历史收盘价、应用设置）均存储在您设备上的应用私有容器内，路径为 Application Support/AssetBoard。
  • 桌面与锁屏小组件通过 iOS 的 App Group 机制读取同一份本地数据快照。该共享仅发生在您的设备内部，不会离开设备。
  • 上述数据受 iOS 系统级加密保护；设备处于锁定状态时无法读取。
  • 当前版本不提供跨设备同步，未启用 iCloud 或任何云端存储。

当前版本不提供任何内购或订阅。若未来版本推出订阅服务，购买与支付将完全由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们不会接触、记录或存储您的支付信息。

我们不出售、不出租、不共享、不转让您的任何数据。由于我们在技术上无法访问您设备上的数据，也不存在向第三方提供数据的可能性。

六、数据保留与删除

  • 保留期限：数据仅存在于您的设备上，保留多久完全由您决定。
  • 删除方式：您可在应用内删除单条持仓或成交记录，或使用设置中的清空功能；卸载本应用将一并清除全部数据。

Privacy Policy (English)

In one sentence: we do not collect any of your data. AssetBoard is a local-first portfolio tracker. We have no server and operate no backend service of any kind. Your holdings, trade records and asset data stay on your own device — we cannot access them and do not transmit them to anyone.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The app requires no sign-up or login and does not collect information that could identify you, directly or indirectly. Specifically, we do not:

  • collect your name, phone number, email address or any government identifier;
  • collect device identifiers (such as IDFV or IDFA), or track you across apps or websites;
  • integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK;
  • request sensitive system permissions such as location, camera, photos, microphone, contacts or notifications (no such permission is declared by the app);
  • collect your holdings, transactions, cost basis, total asset value or any other financial data.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • All data (holdings, trade records, historical closing prices and app settings) is stored inside the app's private container on your device, at Application Support/AssetBoard.
  • The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read the same local snapshot through Apple's App Group mechanism. That sharing happens entirely within your device and never leaves it.
  • This data is protected by iOS system-level encryption and cannot be read while the device is locked.
  • The current version offers no cross-device sync and does not use iCloud or any cloud storage.

3. Network Requests and Third-Party Data Sources

To display live market quotes, the app needs network access. Requests contain only query parameters such as security codes and date ranges. They contain no personally identifying information and never upload your holdings. Your device contacts the following public endpoints directly, using your own network IP address:

4. Purchases and Payments

The current version offers no in-app purchases or subscriptions. If a subscription is introduced in a future version, all purchases and payments will be handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We will not receive, record or store your payment information.

5. Sharing, Selling and Cross-Border Transfer

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone. Because we technically cannot access the data on your device, providing it to a third party is not possible.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Retention: your data exists only on your device, for as long as you choose.
  • Deletion: you can delete individual holdings or trades in the app, or use the clear-data function in Settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data.
  • We cannot delete or export data on your behalf, because we hold no copy of it.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable laws such as the GDPR and PIPL, you have the right to access, correct, delete, port and withdraw consent regarding your personal data. Because the app collects no data, you exercise these rights directly on your own device (the app provides viewing, editing, deletion and export). If you need any assistance, please contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
